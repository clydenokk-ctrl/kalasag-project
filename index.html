Executive Overview

\
\

Kalasag Interdisciplinary Enterprise

\

\ Kalasag\'97derived from the Kapampangan word for "shield"\'97is an agricultural technology enterprise that integrates applied physics and biological sustainability to protect rural farming communities[cite: 2]. Kalasag executes its dual-impact mandate through two tailored innovations:\

\
\
    \
  • Project KAPYOT (Physics Branch): Applies mechanical force-dissipation and rotational kinetics principles to prevent high-altitude spinal trauma among coconut harvesters[cite: 2].
    • \
  • Gut-o ko to! (Biology Branch): Leverages targeted bio-fermentation to mitigate gastrointestinal dysbiosis through zero-waste probiotic nutrition[cite: 2].

PROJECT KAPYOT: Fall-Arrest Inertial Reel Lock & Pneumatic Spinal Air-Cushion

\ \ \
\ I. Design Proposal\

Project KAPYOT (Centrifugal Inertial Reel Lock & Pneumatic Air-Cushion)

\

\ Derived from the Visayan/Tagalog word "kapyot" (meaning "to hold on or cling tightly for safety"), Project KAPYOT is a lightweight, wearable safety harness engineered specifically for Filipino coconut harvesters (manggigiri or magsasaka) who climb vertical coconut trees up to 60 to 100 feet daily without any fallback protection[cite: 2].\

\ \

Core Motion Safety Systems

\
    \
  • The Yo-Yo Tensioner (Vertical Centrifugal Lock): Secured to the climber\'92s waist and wrapped dynamically around the tree trunk using an abaca-reinforced polymer strap[cite: 2]. As the climber moves at normal speeds, a high-tensile steel cable freely spools out or retracts[cite: 2]. If a slip occurs, the sudden acceleration (≥ 0.3g) triggers internal centrifugal flyweights to swing outward, instantly locking the reel within centimeters[cite: 2].
    • \
  • The Pneumatic Spinal Air-Cushion: If the rapid lock is engaged, the sudden tension spike in the cable mechanically punctures a mini compressed CO₂ cartridge[cite: 2]. Within 0.08 seconds, a high-strength nylon cushion inflates along the lumbar-thoracic (spinal) region, preventing the spine from slamming against the tree bark[cite: 2].
    • \
\ \ \
\ \ \

Figure 1. Mechanical Component Integration and Fall-Arrest Activation Diagram for Project KAPYOT[cite: 2].

\

Target Community & Novelty

\
    \
  • Target Community: Millions of small-scale Filipino coconut farmers (manggigiri) across rural provinces (Quezon, Leyte, Bicol, Davao) where palm falls are a primary cause of paralysis and fatalities[cite: 2].
    • \
  • Novelty: Re-engineers high-speed horizontal automotive safety mechanisms for vertical, gravity-dominated agricultural scenarios[cite: 2].
    • \
  • Affordability: Entirely mechanical (centrifugal force & spring tension), making it waterproof, highly durable, and cost-effective[cite: 2].
    • \
  • Local Material Integration: Utilizes locally woven Abaca fibers (Musa textilis) for high friction resistance and extreme tensile strength[cite: 2].

\ III. Mathematical Analysis & Physics Computation\

Kinematic & Centrifugal Lock Computations

\

\ Problem Statement: A 70-kg coconut harvester slips from a palm trunk[cite: 2]. The reel has a drum radius $R = 0.05\\text\{ m\}$ ($5\\text\{ cm\}$) and internal flyweights of mass $m_f = 0.02\\text\{ kg\}$ located at radius $r = 0.03\\text\{ m\}$[cite: 2]. The flyweight retaining spring requires a force $F_s = 4.80\\text\{ N\}$ to allow outward displacement[cite: 2]. Assuming free-fall acceleration ($g = 9.80\\text\{ m/s\}^2$) and initial velocity $v_0 = 0\\text\{ m/s\}$[cite: 2]:\

\ \
\ 1. CRITICAL ACTIVATION VELOCITY\ Centrifugal Force Equation: F_c = m_f * (ω_lock)^2 * r = F_s[cite: 2]\ (0.02 kg) * (ω_lock)^2 * (0.03 m) = 4.80 N[cite: 2]\ 0.0006 * (ω_lock)^2 = 4.80 N[cite: 2]\ (ω_lock)^2 = 4.80 / 0.0006 = 8000 rad^2/s^2[cite: 2]\ ω_lock = √8000 = 89.44 rad/s[cite: 2]\ \ Linear Cable Velocity (v_lock):\ v_lock = R * ω_lock = (0.05 m) * (89.44 rad/s)\ v_lock = 4.47 m/s[cite: 2]\ \ 2. REACTION TIME AND VERTICAL DISPLACEMENT\ Elapsed Time (t_lock):\ v_lock = v_0 + g * t_lock[cite: 2]\ 4.47 m/s = 0 + (9.80 m/s^2) * t_lock[cite: 2]\ t_lock = 4.47 / 9.80 = 0.456 seconds[cite: 2]\ \ Vertical Fall Distance (y_lock):\ (v_lock)^2 = (v_0)^2 + 2 * g * y_lock[cite: 2]\ (4.47 m/s)^2 = 0 + 2 * (9.80 m/s^2) * y_lock[cite: 2]\ 19.98 = 19.60 * y_lock[cite: 2]\ y_lock = 19.98 / 19.60 = 1.02 meters[cite: 2]\
\

\ Conclusion: The mechanism engages after a vertical drop of approximately 1.02 meters (within 0.46 seconds), stopping the climber before dangerous momentum accumulates[cite: 2].\

Biology Section

\

Cellular Homeostasis, Gastrointestinal Dysbiosis & Functional Probiotic Innovation

\ \ \
\ I. Cellular Machinery & Homeostasis\

The Intricate Cellular Machinery

\

\ Cells are the basic structural and functional units of all living organisms[cite: 2]. Human tissues rely on specialized intracellular membrane-bound organelles to sustain systemic homeostasis[cite: 2]:\

\
\
    \
  • Plasma Membrane (Phospholipid Bilayer): A selectively permeable amphipathic barrier regulating nutrient influx and waste efflux via active and passive transport[cite: 2].
    • \
  • Mitochondria: Executes oxidative phosphorylation and the Krebs cycle to produce Adenosine Triphosphate (ATP)[cite: 2].
    • \
  • Tight Junctions (Zona Occludens): Specialized protein networks (claudins and occludins) sealing neighboring epithelial cells (enterocytes) to prevent paracellular leakage of toxins and pathogens[cite: 2].
    • \
  • Active Transport (Na+/K+ ATPase Pump): Expels 3 Na+ ions for every 2 K+ ions imported, maintaining cellular volume, membrane potential, and osmotic balance[cite: 2].
    • \
\
\ \ \
\ II. Disease Focus: Gastrointestinal Dysbiosis\

Cellular & Molecular Breakdown of Dysbiosis

\

\ Gastrointestinal Dysbiosis is a disruption in the composition and function of the gut microbiota, marked by a loss of microbial diversity and depletion of beneficial anaerobic bacteria (Lactobacillus, Bifidobacterium)[cite: 2].\

\
\
    \
  1. Short-Chain Fatty Acid (SCFA) Depletion: Diets low in fiber reduce microbial fermentation into SCFAs (butyrate, acetate, propionate)[cite: 2]. Butyrate is the primary energy substrate for colonocytes; its loss impairs mitochondrial ATP production[cite: 2].
    2. \
  2. Mitochondrial Dysfunction & ROS Generation: Cellular stress induces excessive Reactive Oxygen Species (ROS), causing oxidative damage to cell membranes[cite: 2].
    3. \
  3. Tight Junction Breakdown: ROS and elevated pro-inflammatory cytokines (TNF-α, IL-6) downregulate claudin and occludin expression, causing "leaky gut" where endotoxins (LPS) enter the bloodstream[cite: 2].
    4. \

\ III. Functional Solution: "Gut-o ko to!"\

Product Plan & Formulations

\

\ \'93Gut-o ko to!\'94 is an artisanal fermented functional food product crafted from locally grown Philippine Mustard Greens (Brassica juncea / Mustasa) fermented with rice starch washings (hugas-bigas) and coarse sea salt[cite: 2].\

\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \
IngredientBiological / Chemical RoleLocal Philippine Sourcing
Mustard Greens (Mustasa)[cite: 2]Rich in dietary fiber and glucosinolates (precursors to sulforaphane)[cite: 2].Local farms in Central Luzon (Pampanga & Benguet)[cite: 2].
Rice Washings (Hugas-Bigas)[cite: 2]Provides residual starch carbohydrates for fermentation microbes[cite: 2].Kitchen byproduct during daily home rice preparation[cite: 2].
Coarse Sea Salt[cite: 2]Creates selective saline environment favoring lactic acid bacteria[cite: 2].Coastal salt producers in Pangasinan or Zambales[cite: 2].
Garlic & Ginger[cite: 2]Contains organosulfur compounds (alliin) and gingerols for bioactivity[cite: 2].Local wet markets (palengke) across Luzon[cite: 2].
\ \

Preventative Mechanisms

\
    \
  • Colonocyte Support: Fermentation of fiber yields butyrate, restoring mitochondrial ATP synthesis in colon epithelial cells[cite: 2].
    • \
  • Barrier Integrity: Lactic acid metabolites activate Toll-like Receptors (TLR-2), upregulating Claudin-1, Occludin, and ZO-1 tight junction expression[cite: 2].
    • \
  • Antioxidant Nrf2 Activation: Isothiocyanates (sulforaphane) activate the Nrf2 cellular pathway, stimulating antioxidant enzymes to neutralize ROS[cite: 2].
    • \
